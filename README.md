# Halal Screen

A small web app that checks whether a Tadawul or US stock passes the AAOIFI Shariah screens (Shariah Standard No. 21):

- Main business is permissible
- Interest-bearing debt under 30% of market cap
- Interest-bearing cash and securities under 30% of market cap
- Non-permissible income under 5% of revenue

Each check shows the ratio, the limit, the math and a plain-English reason, plus a purification calculator.

Figures are entered from a company's annual report. US tickers can be auto-filled with a free [Financial Modeling Prep](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/) key, which stays in the visitor's browser.

Educational tool, not a fatwa.
